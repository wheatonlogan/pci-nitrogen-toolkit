# PCI brand: quick reference for this app
Generated by the pci-web-app skill from brand kit v2.0.0. Kit-owned. Re-run `scaffold.mjs --update` to refresh.

## Must
- Tokens, never raw hex: `var(--pci-cobalt-blue)`, `var(--ink-primary)`, `var(--surface-1)`, `var(--series-1)`.
- Roboto 300 / 400 / 500 from Google Fonts. Never Neufile Grotesk or Mr Eaves on the web.
- Body text #212121 (`--ink-primary`), not black.
- Industry accent (`--industry-accent`): icons, bars and rules only, ≤6% of the screen, never text on light.
- One `.pci-stat--hero` and one `.pci-btn--primary` per view.
- Charts through PCIChart: ≤4 series (5 extended, 3 scatter or map), no dual axes, a table view under every chart.
- Headlines in upper-and-lower case. Caps only for eyebrow labels.
- App CSS scoped under `.pci-app`. Never edit files in this folder.
- Focus rings stay, real `<table>` markup, labels wrap inputs, 40px hit targets.

## Industry
Set `data-industry` on <body> or a wrapper: water · health · industrial (mining) · energy · food-ag · chemical · aerospace (defense). Omit it for cross-industry nitrogen (Air Blue).
